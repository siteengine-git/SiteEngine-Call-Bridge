calls.qualification_threshold_sec, number_leases.pool_group. No backfill query.

or renewal policy. The first successful Voice INSERT captures canonical-bound
number/lease/session facts and an explicit server UTC observation. Original flat
acquisition and Consent-reference copying remains unchanged. Old lease pools stay
unknown rather than falling back to the number's current pool.

outcome. Historical terminal callbacks remain no-ops. Original naive timestamps
are not relabelled UTC. Validated persisted capture fills evidence-v1 assignment
fields; additive call.capture exposes version/captured_at_utc/lease clock. Invalid
capture returns capture_invalid with no row or diagnostics. Consent is still a
reference, not an authoritative binding or advertising permission.
